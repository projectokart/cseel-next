### Accessing the Admin Portal:
1. Open [`/admin`](http://localhost:3000/admin) on the browser.
2. Enter the Email and Password for any desired department or click any **1-Click Quick Demo Badge**.
3. Super Admins can simulate/switch into any department directly from the top header navigation.
